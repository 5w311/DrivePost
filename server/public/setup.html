Share from iPhone

Add the Send to Car Shortcut once. After that, tap Share in YouTube, Safari or any app and pick Send to Car.

  1. Tap Get the Shortcut. Your personal link is copied, and Safari asks to download a file. Tap Download.
  2. Open the download: tap the download arrow by Safari's address bar, then Send to Car. It's also in the Files app, under Downloads.
  3. Shortcuts asks for your DriveTest link. Tap the box, tap Paste, then Add Shortcut.
Get the Shortcut

This page is open from your Home Screen. If no download starts, open it in Safari instead and tap the button there.

Make it by hand instead
  1. Open Shortcuts, make a new Shortcut, and name it Send to Car.
  2. Tap the ⓘ button and turn on Show in Share Sheet. Set it to receive URLs and Text.
  3. Add the action Get Contents of URL and set it up like this:
    URL (your personal link)
    …
    Method
    POST
    Request Body
    JSON, with a Text field named url set to Shortcut Input
  4. Add Show Notification and set its text to Contents of URL, so you see what was added.

The first time you share, iOS asks whether the Shortcut may connect to your server: tap Always Allow. In the YouTube app, tap Share and then More to find Send to Car. If nothing is playing, a shared video starts right away. Otherwise it goes to Up Next.

Share from Android

  1. Open the remote in Chrome.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install.
  3. Now DriveTest shows up in the share menu of YouTube and other apps.

Check the server

The test pattern is made on your server without YouTube. If it plays in the car but YouTube videos don't, the problem is yt-dlp, not the player.

Security

A new share token changes your personal link, so the Shortcut stops working until you paste the new link into it (open Send to Car in Shortcuts and replace the address). Signing out everywhere signs out the car and every phone.
