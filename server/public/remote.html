Quality

Higher quality uses more data. If the car can't keep up, it steps down by itself for that video.

Video format

H.264 plays the video as YouTube (or the site it came from) made it: sharper, usually less data, and almost no work for your server. If the car can't play it, or a video has no H.264 version, DrivePost uses MPEG-1 by itself. MPEG-1 always converts on your server, which works in any browser.

Car screen

Fullscreen fills the car's browser window while a video plays.
