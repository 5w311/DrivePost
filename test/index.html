Drive Test

Finds out which way of playing video keeps going after the car leaves Park.

  1. In Park, tap Start. Both tiles should say PLAYING.
  2. Foot on the brake, shift into D. You don't need to move.
  3. Wait 10 seconds and note which tiles still say PLAYING.
  4. Shift back to P. Snap a photo of the log below.
